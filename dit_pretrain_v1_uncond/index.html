Latent DiT — pretrain stage, samples from dit_pretrain_v1/final

The 397.6M-parameter latent DiT trained for 120K steps (global batch 384) on the pretrain corpus in the window-VAE latent space of the v2 latent store (0.5 s hop, two 128-d latents per second, rectified flow, 192 s canvas), sampled from the final EMA weights with 50 Euler steps and no classifier-free guidance, and decoded parity-selected through the grammar-constrained decoder inference/latent_decode.py.

The pretrain stage is conditioned on the global fields — key, tempo class, section plan, completeness — with null attributes and an empty sentiment context, so each sample borrows its key, tempo class, section plan and length from a pretrain-validation piece and only the content is sampled. The plan is drawn only inside the roll, as translucent colored spans carrying the section letter.
