Text2MIDI: controllable piano generation

Autoregressive piano generators trained from scratch on MIDI tokens, then fine-tuned to follow text and attribute controls. Each page below is one model checkpoint: five hand-written prompts, three random seeds each, rendered to audio with a synced piano-roll so you can hear and see how the music follows the prompt.

Weak SFT — 0.89B generator

final weak-SFT checkpoint

The current conditional model: a 0.89B MIDI-native decoder pretrained on piano performances, then fine-tuned on 371K weakly-labeled pieces. Controls enter as a key/tempo header, four attribute tags (emotion, genre, accompaniment, performance style) and a free-text sentiment sentence encoded by Qwen3-Embedding-4B.

  • Checkpoint midi_gen_weak_sft_v2/final
  • Resolution 25 ms, 4,096-token context
  • Sentiment encoder Qwen3-Embedding-4B (2560-d)
  • Sampling T 0.98 · top-p 0.98

How to read a demo page.
  • The left sidebar lists the five prompts; each card shows the attribute tags, key, tempo class and the sentiment text the model was given.
  • Tabs on a card switch between the three seeds. Press play, or click anywhere in the roll to seek.
  • Translucent colored spans in the roll are the model's own section plan (A, B, C …, 5 s windows); the orange strip is the sustain pedal.
