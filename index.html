Text2MIDI: controllable piano generation

Autoregressive piano generators trained from scratch on MIDI tokens, then fine-tuned to follow text and attribute controls. Each page below is one model checkpoint: five hand-written prompts, three random seeds each, rendered to audio with a synced piano-roll so you can hear and see how the music follows the prompt.

Latent DiT — weak stage, conditional samples

latent DiT

397.2M-parameter DiT trained for 120K steps on the 371K weak corpus in the window-VAE latent space, generating one 128-d latent per second with rectified flow on a 192 s canvas, then decoded back to MIDI under the token grammar. Same five prompts and seeds as the Middle SFT page; unlike the AR model the DiT takes the section plan and the piece length as a time-aligned input, so each prompt borrows those from a Lyria validation piece with the same emotion and genre.

  • Sentiment encoder Qwen3-Embedding-4B (2560-d), cross-attention
  • Sampling EMA weights · CFG 3.0 · 50 Euler steps, seeds 101–103

Window VAE reconstruction — 12 pieces, 4 per stage

latent tokenizer check

The collaborator's MuseTok-style window VAE (one 128-d latent per second, 12-layer encoder/decoder) that the planned latent DiT will generate into. Each card plays a piece's original token stream next to its reconstruction from the latents, decoded free-running per 20 s chunk with a token-grammar mask; note F1 0.94 against the original at ±50 ms, velocity 99.6 % and duration 99.3 % agreement.

  • Latent 1 per second, 128-d
  • Pieces 4 weak · 4 middle · 4 Lyria at note-density quantiles
  • Decoding constrained greedy, chunked 20 s
